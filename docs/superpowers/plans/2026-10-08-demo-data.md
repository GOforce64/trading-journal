# Demo Data and README Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** `pnpm demo` opens the journal on six months of deterministic fake trades, with synthetic bars so every chart works without a key; the README shows that demo and gains an architecture diagram, run-it-yourself steps and CONTRIBUTING.

**Architecture:**
- A new pure package, `packages/demo` (`@tj/demo`), plans prices and trades from a seeded PRNG. It then writes them through the app's own repositories (trades, taxonomy, bars, scalp prices) in one transaction.
- `apps/server/src/demo.ts` makes a guarded throwaway data directory, runs the generator, then starts the normal server (`index.ts`) on it.
- The README's images come from that demo.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Drizzle and better-sqlite3 (through `@tj/db`), `@tj/core`'s calendar and Black-Scholes, Vitest, puppeteer-core with headless Firefox (for the screenshots only, from the scratchpad).

**Spec:** [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-demo-data-design.md](../specs/2026-10-08-demo-data-design.md)

## Global Constraints

- **`@tj/demo` imports only `@tj/core`, `@tj/db` and its own files:** no `node:` modules outside tests, and no `Date.now()` or `Math.random()`. Time comes from `options.now`, randomness from the seeded PRNG.
- **Defaults:**
  - seed 42, six months;
  - port 4179 (`TJ_PORT` overrides);
  - directory `<os tmpdir>/trading-journal-demo` (`TJ_DEMO_DIR` overrides), marked by a `.tj-demo` file.
- **Sessions:** the regular session only, from 09:30 to `regularClose(date)`, on `isTradingDay` dates.
- **Bar coverage:**
  - daily bars and days from 1,095 calendar days before the first session through the end;
  - minute days for every calendar day from 7 days before each trade's entry through its exit (empty on non-sessions);
  - each scalp contract's minute bars on its trade day, and known empty days for the rest of its range.
- **Symbols:**
  - scalps on SPY, QQQ, NVDA, TSLA;
  - flies on NFLX, ORCL, MU, NKE, FDX, ADBE, COST, AVGO, CRM, LULU.
- **Books:** live and paper only. Every trade is closed, `source: "manual"`.
- **Fees:** $0.65 a contract a side plus $0.02 a contract.
- **Tests stay light:** no test generates more than two months or writes more than a few MB, since a heavy test slowed Windows CI about 20× on PR #18.

## Review Focus

1. **The calendar inside the period:** holidays and half days (Nov 27 and Dec 24, 2026 close at 13:00). No trade falls on a closed day, a half day's bars stop at 13:00, and non-sessions in a chart range are known-empty days.
2. **Every chart range is covered,** so a demo trade's minute, daily and option charts never answer `no_key`.
3. **The demo directory guard:** `pnpm demo` never wipes the real data directory, or any directory without the `.tj-demo` marker.
4. **The default end date** when today is a weekend, a holiday, or a session before the close: the last *finished* session before today. An `--end` of today or later is refused.
5. **P&L consistency:** each trade's `netPnl` equals its legs' `(close − open) × quantity × 100` less its fees, for both strategies, and fly credits are positive.

Each one gets its test in the task that owns the code (Tasks 2, 5 and 6).

    let a = seed >>> 0;
    return () => {
      a = (a + 0x6d2b79f5) >>> 0;
      let t = a;
      t = Math.imul(t ^ (t >>> 15), t | 1);
      t ^= t + Math.imul(t ^ (t >>> 7), t | 61);
      return ((t ^ (t >>> 14)) >>> 0) / 4294967296;
    };
  }

### Task 2: Prices — the daily walk, the minute bridge, option bars

**Files:**
- Create: `packages/demo/src/symbols.ts`, `packages/demo/src/prices.ts`, `packages/demo/src/prices.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1's `Rng` helpers; core's `isTradingDay`, `regularClose`, `addDays`, `nyWallClock`, `bsPrice`, `yearsToExpiry`, `RATE`, and `PriceBar` (`{ t, o, h, l, c, v }`).
- Produces:
  - `SYMBOLS: Record<string, { start: number; vol: number; iv: number; volume: number; strikeStep: number; kind: "scalp" | "fly" }>`: starting prices near today's real levels, annual vol, typical IV, a volume scale, and a strike increment (1 for SPY/QQQ, 2.5 or 5 for the rest).
  - `tradingDays(from: string, to: string): string[]`: the `isTradingDay` dates, inclusive. (Not `sessionsBetween`: core already exports that name, counting sessions.)
  - `dailyWalk(rng: Rng, symbol: string, sessions: readonly string[], gaps: ReadonlyMap<string, number>): Map<string, { open: number; close: number }>`: GBM closes with a 7% annual drift. A session in `gaps` opens at the previous close × (1 + gap); any other session opens within ±0.4% of it.
  - `minuteBars(rng: Rng, date: string, open: number, close: number, vol: number, volume: number): PriceBar[]`: from 09:30 to `regularClose(date)`, one bar a minute, `t` = `nyWallClock(date, minute)`. A Brownian bridge from `open` to `close` with the variance weighted ×2 in the first and last 30 minutes. Each bar's `o` is the previous bar's `c` (the first is `open`), `h`/`l` come from 4 sub-steps, the last `c` is exactly `close`, and `v` is U-shaped.
  - `dailyBar(date: string, minutes: readonly PriceBar[]): PriceBar`: aggregated, with `t = nyWallClock(date, 0)`.
  - `syntheticDailyBar(rng, date, open, close, vol): PriceBar`: for a day without minutes, a range of about `vol/√252` around open and close.
  - `optionBars(stock: readonly PriceBar[], contract: { right: "C" | "P"; strike: number; expiry: string }, iv: number, rng: Rng): PriceBar[]`: each bar's o/h/l/c is `max(bsPrice(...), intrinsic + 0.01)` on the stock's o/h/l/c, with IV × (1 ± 2% noise), rounded to the cent. Volume is a small integer.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the tests** (`prices.test.ts`):
  - `tradingDays("2026-11-23", "2026-11-30")` skips the 26th (Thanksgiving) and the weekend;
  - `minuteBars` on 2026-11-27 (a half day) has 210 bars, the last at 12:59; on a full day it has 390;
  - every bar has `l ≤ min(o, c)` and `h ≥ max(o, c)`, the first `o` equals `open`, and the last `c` equals `close`;
  - `dailyBar` aggregates (first open, max high, min low, last close, summed volume);
  - `dailyWalk` with a gap of −0.08 on a date opens that date at the previous close × 0.92, and the same rng seed gives the same map;
  - `optionBars` for a 0DTE call never goes below intrinsic + 0.01, and rises with the stock.

- [ ] **Step 3:** Implement `symbols.ts` and `prices.ts`.
- [ ] **Step 4:** Run. Expected: PASS. Format, lint, typecheck.

### Task 3: Scalp plans

**Files:**
- Create: `packages/demo/src/scalps.ts`, `packages/demo/src/scalps.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Tasks 1–2.
- Produces:
  - `ScalpPlan`:
    `{ symbol; book: "live" | "paper"; right; strike; expiry; contracts; openedAt; closedAt; openPrice; closePrice; fees; netPnl; stop: { basis: "stock" | "premium"; price: number }; targets: { price: number; contracts: number }[]; setup: string; grade: Grade | null; tags: string[]; notes: string | null; reviewed: boolean; optionBars: PriceBar[] }`;
    `optionBars` holds the contract's bars for the day, the very bars that priced the entry and exit, which Task 5 stores;
    setup and tags are taxonomy *names*, resolved to ids when written.
  - `planScalps(rng: Rng, date: string, symbol: string, stock: readonly PriceBar[], reviewed: boolean): ScalpPlan[]`: 0–2 scalps on one symbol's day (the caller picks the symbols for each session per spec §2).
  - `expiryFor(symbol: string, date: string): string`: the same day for SPY and QQQ, otherwise that week's Friday (Thursday when Friday is a holiday).
- **Rules (spec §2):**
  - **Entry:** a minute 09:31–11:00 70% of the time, else 11:00–15:00, at second 0–59.
  - **Direction:** the sign of the next 30 minutes' drift with probability 0.58.
  - **Strike:** one strike step out of the money; 1–5 contracts.
  - **Exit:** walk the option bars (built here with `optionBars` for the day) from the entry. Leave at the first of: the premium up 25–60% (a drawn target), the stock through the stop (0.15–0.35% against the entry), or 5–40 minutes. The exit price is that minute's close.
  - **Levels:** a stock stop in 80% of plans, a premium stop at the stopped premium in 20%; one target at the drawn target price, plus a second 40% of the time when contracts ≥ 2.
  - **Grades** lean with the outcome per spec §2; a third of losers get a mistake tag, and every plan gets an emotion tag.
  - **Notes:** half the plans get one from a 12-entry template list keyed by setup.
  - **`reviewed` false** leaves out the grade 50% of the time and the setup otherwise.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the tests:**
  - plans on a seeded day are identical for the same rng seed;
  - every entry and exit is inside the session, with exit > entry;
  - `netPnl` equals `(closePrice − openPrice) × contracts × 100 − fees` to the cent, with fees = contracts × 2 × 0.67;
  - a stopped plan's exit is at or past its stop;
  - the targets' contracts never exceed the position;
  - `expiryFor("NVDA", "2026-11-23")` is "2026-11-27", and `expiryFor("SPY", "2026-11-23")` is "2026-11-23";
  - over 200 seeded days the win rate lies between 0.45 and 0.65.
- [ ] **Step 2:** Run. Expected: FAIL.
- [ ] **Step 3:** Implement.
- [ ] **Step 4:** Run. Expected: PASS. Format, lint, typecheck.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit:** `feat(demo): scalp plans that enter, exit at a target, stop or time, and carry their review`.

### Task 4: Earnings and fly plans

**Files:**
- Create: `packages/demo/src/flies.ts`, `packages/demo/src/flies.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Tasks 1–2.
- Produces:
  - `EarningsEvent`:
    `{ symbol: string; date: string; timing: "BMO" | "AMC"; entryDate: string; exitDate: string; gap: number; reactionDate: string }`.
    - AMC: entry on `date`, and the reaction and exit on the next session.
    - BMO: entry on the previous session, and the reaction and exit on `date`.
  - `earningsSchedule(rng: Rng, symbol: string, sessions: readonly string[]): EarningsEvent[]`: one event every 13 weeks (± 1 week), offset per symbol, inside `sessions`. The `gap` is normal × 0.9 × the implied move (`iv × √(1/252) × 0.8`).
  - `FlyPlan`:
    `{ symbol; book: "live"; legs: { right; strike; expiry; quantity; openPrice; closePrice }[]; contracts; openedAt; closedAt; fees; netPnl; creditPerShare; netCost; earningsDate; earningsTiming; impliedMovePct; actualMovePct; ivBefore; ivAfter; bodyStrike; putWing; callWing; setup: "Earnings IV crush"; grade; tags; notes }`.
  - `planFly(rng: Rng, event: EarningsEvent, entryStock: readonly PriceBar[], exitStock: readonly PriceBar[]): FlyPlan`:
    - entry at 15:40–15:55 on the entry day, exit at 09:45–10:30 on the exit day;
    - the expiry is the Friday on or after the exit day;
    - the body is the strike nearest the entry price, with the wings at body ± round(1.5 × implied move × price) to the strike step;
    - IV before is drawn from 70–120% and IV after from 30–45%; legs are priced with `bsPrice`;
    - `netPnl = (credit − debit) × contracts × 100 − fees`, with fees = 4 legs × contracts × 2 × 0.67.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the tests:**
  - the events are about 13 weeks apart, all inside the sessions, with BMO and AMC dates laid out as above;
  - a plan's credit is positive and its wings straddle the body;
  - `netPnl` equals the legs' P&L less fees to the cent;
  - `actualMovePct` equals |reaction open ÷ previous close − 1|;
  - over 100 seeded events, a majority but not all are winners.

### Task 5: `generateDemo` — writing it all through the repositories

**Files:**
- Create: `packages/demo/src/write.ts`, `packages/demo/src/index.ts`, `packages/demo/src/generate.test.ts`, `packages/demo/src/journal.test-helper.ts` (a migrated in-memory journal for tests, as `packages/db/src/bundle.fixture.ts` makes one)

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Tasks 1–4; from `@tj/db`: `createTradesRepo(db, now)`, `createTaxonomyRepo(db, now)` (`seedDefaults`, `listSetups`, `listTags`), `createBarsRepo(db).store(symbol, timeframe, days, fetchedAt)`, `trades.update(id, { setupId, grade, tagIds, notes, scalp: { levelBasis, stopPrice, targets } })`, `trades.setScalpPrices(id, { entryPrice, holdHigh, holdLow, optionHigh, optionLow }, fetchedAt)`; from core: `occSymbol`, `stockAt`, `holdRange`.
- Produces:
  - `generateDemo(db: Db, options: { end: string; seed?: number; months?: number; now?: () => number }): DemoSummary`
  - `DemoSummary = { from: string; to: string; scalps: number; flies: number; bars: number }`
  - `lastFinishedSession(now: number): string`: the latest `isTradingDay` date before today's New York date.
- **Flow:**
  1. The sessions are `tradingDays(addDays(end, -Math.round(months × 30.4)), end)`. The earnings schedule comes per fly symbol.
  2. Each symbol's daily walk runs over `tradingDays(addDays(firstSession, -1095), end)`, with its earnings gaps keyed by each event's `reactionDate`.
  3. Scalp days: per session, 60% chance, 1–2 scalp symbols, each through `planScalps`. Scalps are `reviewed` unless they fall in the last three sessions.
  4. Minute bars are made for each (symbol, session) a trade's chart range touches, with `dailyBar` replacing that day's synthetic daily bar.
  5. Store everything:
     - `bars.store` by day, `fetchedAt = now()`;
     - daily: every calendar day from the walk's start through `end`, empty on non-sessions;
     - minutes: every calendar day in the union of chart ranges per symbol, empty on non-sessions;
     - each scalp contract's bars on its own day, and its other range days empty.
  6. `taxonomy.seedDefaults()`, then each trade in time order through `trades.create` with `now` at its close plus 3–30 minutes, then `trades.update` for levels and annotations, then `setScalpPrices` from `stockAt` and `holdRange` on the stored minutes (option range from the option bars).
  7. All inside `db.$client.transaction`. A repository refusal throws `Error("demo trade refused: <symbol> <openedAt ISO>: <cause>")`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the tests** (`generate.test.ts`, `months: 1`, `end: "2026-12-31"`, so Thanksgiving week and Dec 24 fall inside):

  - each trade's `netPnl` equals its legs' P&L less fees (Review Focus 5);
  - no trade opens on a non-session, and Dec 24's minute bars stop at 13:00 (Review Focus 1);
  - for every trade, `bars.knownDays(symbol, "1m", every date of its chart range)` holds them all, and `knownDays(symbol, "1d", 1,095 days back)` too (Review Focus 2, db side);
  - two runs on two fresh journals give the same trades (underlying, openedAt, closedAt, netPnl, legs), ignoring ids;
  - scalps older than three sessions pass `reviewStatus` as done, and at least one recent one is pending;
  - every scalp has scalp prices;
  - `lastFinishedSession` gives:
    - Friday 2026-11-27 for Saturday 2026-11-28 at noon;
    - Wednesday 2026-11-25 for Thanksgiving (2026-11-26) at noon;
    - Monday 2026-11-30 for Tuesday 2026-12-01 at 10:00 New York, a session not yet finished (Review Focus 4).
- [ ] **Step 2:** Run. Expected: FAIL.
